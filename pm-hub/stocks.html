📖 How to Use the Stock Screener

🎯 What is Williams %R?

Williams %R (Williams Percent Range) measures overbought/oversold conditions on a -100 to 0 scale.

-80 to -100
🟢 Oversold — BUY ZONE
-50 to -80
🟡 Neutral Zone
0 to -50
🔴 Overbought — SELL ZONE

📊 What is RSI?

Relative Strength Index (RSI) measures price momentum on a 0-100 scale. Traditional thresholds:

Below 30
🟢 Oversold
30–70
🟡 Neutral
Above 70
🔴 Overbought

💡 Munger-Style Value Strategy

Charlie Munger and Warren Buffett look for "margin of safety" — buying quality assets when they're on sale.

  • • Oversold doesn't always mean "buy now" — check fundamentals
  • • Look for stocks that are historically oversold but have strong underlying business
  • • Dollar-cost average into positions over time, not all at once
  • • Combine with your own research — this is a screening tool, not financial advice

⚠️ Important Limitations

  • • Technical indicators are lagging — they tell you what happened, not what will happen
  • • Williams %R can stay oversold/overbought for extended periods in strong trends
  • • This tool uses free Yahoo Finance data — may have 15-20 min delays
  • • Not financial advice. Always do your own due diligence.
